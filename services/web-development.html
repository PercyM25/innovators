    How it works

    From first brief to website handover

    We agree the requirements, responsibilities and milestones before development starts.

    1

    Share your requirements

    Tell us about your business, audience, goals, content and required pages, screens or functionality. Use the estimator as a starting point.

    2

    Review and quotation

    We clarify the scope, discuss suitable options and provide a written quotation with the confirmed price, deposit, milestones and expected delivery date.

    3

    Build and review

    After approval and the agreed deposit, work proceeds through development and client review. Feedback and content are handled against the agreed scope.

    4

    Test and refine

    We review the agreed functionality, check the project with you and resolve in-scope adjustments before handover.

    5

    Approve and settle

    You approve the agreed deliverables and complete the remaining payment according to the written quotation.

    6

    Delivery and handover

    We provide the completed website or application and agreed access or handover information. Launch, hosting, training and ongoing support are included only when quoted.
