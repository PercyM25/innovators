Our Service

Graphic Design

Service Overview

Visual communication with purpose

About This Service

THE INNOVATORS provides creative and professional graphic design services that help individuals, entrepreneurs, small businesses and organisations communicate their brands effectively. We transform ideas into attractive, meaningful and professional designs that capture attention, communicate clearly and create a strong brand identity.

Our work includes business branding, logo creation, poster and flyer design, social media graphics, business cards, company profiles and other marketing materials.

What We Offer

  • Business branding and visual identity
  • Basic and professional logo design
  • Posters, flyers and business cards
  • Social media posts and design packages
  • Company profile and marketing material design

Client Benefits

Professional design helps establish a credible image, improve brand recognition, attract potential customers and promote products or services. We create original, simple, attractive and consistent designs that reflect your business and help you stand out from competitors while building customer trust.

Problems We Help Solve

We help clients who need attractive marketing materials, a consistent brand identity or stronger promotional content for digital platforms. Our designs make key messages easier to understand and give businesses a polished, cohesive presence across their marketing channels.

Our Design Process

1. Brief and discovery

We discuss your needs, business objectives, target audience, preferred colours and project requirements.

2. Concept development

We develop suitable concepts, select appropriate colours and fonts and create an initial design direction.

3. Review and revision

You review the concept and may request reasonable revisions within the agreed project scope.

4. Final delivery

After approval and payment are confirmed, we prepare and deliver the final artwork in the agreed formats.

Final File Formats

Standard final designs can be supplied in PNG, JPG and PDF formats. Other formats are available upon request, subject to project requirements and feasibility.

How to Order

  1. Contact THE INNOVATORS and describe the design you need.
  2. Share your content, preferred colours, text, images and intended audience.
  3. We confirm the project scope, selected package or design, price and estimated delivery date.
  4. Once the project terms are agreed, we begin the design process.
  5. Review the first concept and request reasonable revisions within the agreed scope.
  6. Approve the final design and receive the agreed files.

Payment Terms

Payment terms, including any required deposit and remaining balance, are confirmed in writing with your quotation. Additional costs must be agreed before extra work is undertaken. Final artwork is released after payment and approval have been completed.

Delivery Process

The delivery date depends on the project's complexity and timely receipt of approved content and feedback. We provide progress or preview files for review, keep the agreed revisions within project scope and send the final high-quality files through the approved delivery method after the design is approved and payment is confirmed.
